Cash out guide

Cash out to your bank

You were paid in a digital version of your local money on Celo. Here's how to turn it into money in your bank account.

Last checked 21 November 2026. Apps change what they support, so check inside the app before you send.

DocsCash outPrivacyTerms

Before you send anything

Read first
  • Send a small test amount first. Wait until it arrives on the other side, then send the rest.
  • Only send on a network the app lists for that token. If the receiving app doesn't show “Celo” for that exact token, don't send it there. It can be lost.
  • Check the token address, not just the name. Different coins can have similar names. Compare the address below with the one your wallet shows.
  • Lokal never asks for your seed phrase. No real support team does. Anyone who asks for it is trying to take your money.
Using MiniPay? MiniPay shows only USDT, USDC and USDm. Local tokens (cNGN, IDRX, wARS, wCOP) sent to a MiniPay address are on the blockchain, but they won't appear in the app. If you cash out through MiniPay, receive USD₮ or swap to USD₮ first.

Naira · cNGN

Via USD₮
cNGN contract on Celo
0xF6829D7393dAe24509eb1E52eE8e572e2E271a4f
6 decimals · listed in the issuer's docs

No Nigerian exchange confirms cNGN deposits on the Celo network, so the route goes through dollars.

  1. Swap cNGN to USD₮

    Swap on Celo, for example in the Uniswap cNGN/USDT pool. The pool is small, so check the price impact before you confirm.

  2. Withdraw with MiniPay

    Send the USD₮ (Celo network) to MiniPay and tap Withdraw. MiniPay lists Nigerian bank and mobile money cash-out through Fonbnk Instant Pay.

  3. Test first

    Run the whole route with a small amount. Once it reaches your bank, repeat with the rest.

  • FeesSwap: pool fee, price impact and a little CELO for gas. Withdraw: set by MiniPay's partner and shown before you confirm.
  • ID check (KYC)Set by MiniPay's partner
  • TimingNot published. The app shows it before you confirm.
Watch out
  • Don't send Celo cNGN to Busha, Quidax or Roqqu. Busha lists cNGN deposits as “No” and no Celo network; Quidax lists other networks; Roqqu supports Base only.
  • Yellow Card closed its retail app on 1 January 2026.
  • Redeeming with the issuer needs a verified cNGN merchant account. It isn't a route for individuals.
  • Mento's naira token, NGNm (0xE2702Bd9…6F71), is a different coin. Check the address.

Rupiah · IDRX

Official route
IDRX contract on Celo
0x18Bc5bcC660cf2B9cE3cd51a404aFe1a0cBD3C22
2 decimals · listed in the issuer's docs

IDRX buys back its own token for rupiah, paid to your bank. This is the issuer's documented route.

  1. Sign up

    Go to home.idrx.co, create an account and verify with your KTP and a selfie.

  2. Redeem

    Open the Redeem tab, connect your wallet, choose the Celo network, enter the amount and a bank account in your own name.

  3. Sign the burn

    Approve it in your wallet. Rupiah arrives by BI-FAST, usually within 24 hours.

  • FeesRp5,000 below Rp250M. Rp35,000 from Rp250M (RTGS, weekdays 08:00–15:00 WIB).
  • MinimumRp20,000
  • ID check (KYC)KTP and selfie
  • TimingUsually within 24 hours
  • Helpsupport@idrx.co
Watch out
  • Only burn IDRX on the IDRX site. Burning it anywhere else loses the money.
  • A burn can't be undone. If it isn't confirmed within 2 hours it's marked FAILED: contact support.
  • Indodax lists IDRX on Base only, not Celo. To use Indodax, bridge to Base through IDRX first (flat 10,000 IDRX fee).

Argentine peso · wARS

Not officially confirmed
wARS contract on Celo
0x0DC4F92879B7670e5f4e4e6e3c801D229129D90D
18 decimals · issued by Ripio

Ripio launched its wFIAT tokens on Celo on 31 July 2026, but its wARS page still lists only Ethereum, World Chain and Base. One user report (29 October 2026) saw Ripio accept wARS on Celo. Ripio hasn't confirmed it.

  1. Find the deposit

    In Ripio, tap Depositar, then wARS. Choose the Celo network only if Ripio offers it. If it doesn't, stop and use the fallback below.

  2. Send a test

    Send a small amount to that address and wait until it shows in your Ripio balance.

  3. Withdraw pesos

    Withdraw to your CBU, CVU, alias or Mercado Pago. Then send the rest the same way.

Fallback: swap wARS to USD₮ on Celo, send it to MiniPay and tap Withdraw. MiniPay lists El Dorado for Argentina.

  • Fees0% to withdraw pesos, per Ripio
  • ID check (KYC)A verified Ripio account
  • Timing24–48 business hours, historically
  • Helpsoporte@ripio.com
Watch out
  • The Celo deposit isn't officially confirmed. Never send if Ripio doesn't show Celo for wARS.
  • Ripio's wFIAT tokens aren't legal tender and have no deposit insurance.

Colombian peso · wCOP

No direct route
wCOP contract on Celo
0x8a1D45e102e886510e891d2Ec656a708991e2D76
18 decimals · issued by Ripio

No exchange confirms wCOP on Celo, and you have no right to redeem it with the issuer. Of the four local tokens, it's the hardest to cash out.

  1. Swap wCOP to USD₮

    Swap on Celo if a route exists. Liquidity is thin: if there's no route or the price is poor, stop.

  2. Withdraw with MiniPay

    Send the USD₮ (Celo network) to MiniPay and tap Withdraw. MiniPay lists El Dorado for Colombian banks.

  3. Or ask Ripio

    If you can't swap, write to Ripio support at soporte@ripio.com and ask what they can do.

  • FeesSwap: pool fee and price impact. Withdraw: set by El Dorado and shown before you confirm.
  • ID check (KYC)Set by MiniPay's partner
  • TimingNot published
Watch out
  • The issuer doesn't buy wCOP back: its whitepaper denies a redemption right (per Pharos).
  • Don't send wCOP to an exchange. None confirms it on Celo.
  • It's the least liquid of the four. Swap a small amount first and check what you get.
  • Ripio's wFIAT tokens aren't legal tender and have no deposit insurance.

Dollars · USD₮

Fallback for all
USD₮ contract on Celo
0x48065fbBE25f71C9282ddf5e1cD6D6A887483D5e
6 decimals · Tether

USD₮ is the fallback for every currency above: more apps accept it on Celo than any local token.

  1. Send to MiniPay

    Send the USD₮ to your MiniPay address on the Celo network. It shows up there as USDT.

  2. Withdraw

    Tap Withdraw and pick a partner for your country, bank or mobile money. The fee and what you'll get are shown first.

  3. Test first

    Try a small amount, check your bank, then send the rest.

  • FeesSet by MiniPay's partner in your country
  • ID check (KYC)Set by the partner
  • TimingShown in the app before you confirm
Watch out
  • MiniPay cash-out in Indonesia isn't confirmed. For rupiah, use the IDRX route.
  • Binance has supported USDT on Celo since April 2024, but has had no naira since 2024. After an October 2026 incident it was reopening withdrawals in phases, so Celo withdrawals are unconfirmed.
  • Bybit: USDT on the Celo network is unconfirmed. Check that the deposit page lists Celo before you send.
  • Selling person to person (P2P)? Release the USD₮ only after the money is in your bank account. Check your bank app, not a screenshot.
