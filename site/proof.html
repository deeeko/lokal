    How each payout is checked

    Read, matched, never typed.

    • The payout

      Every token transfer the agent sent to someone, read from its transactions on Celo. The amount out is taken from the transfer itself.

    • The deposit

      The payment that paid for it: a TagPaid or PotPaid event on the Lokal contracts, or the agent's own record of the deposit. If none is found, the row says so.

    • The attribution tag

      Lokal's ERC-8021 tag celo_7360953d1b53 must sit at the end of the payout's input. A payout without it is marked in red.

    • No addresses

      Payers and recipients are counted, never shown. The transactions are public on Celoscan for anyone who wants to look further.
