Ways to send

  • To a Lokal tag. Type their tag above. You pay USD₮ from your own wallet and they receive naira, rupiah or pesos (or pay cNGN and they get dollars).
  • To a payment link. Open the link you were sent. It shows the exact amount, and you can pay it from your wallet. About payment links
  • Being paid instead? Create a link or claim a Lokal tag for your own wallet.
This site never holds your money or keys. Payments go from your own wallet after you approve them there, and the agent converts on Textile FX and pays the recipient.

A dry run fetches a quote and checks the recipient, then stops. Nothing is sent. With --execute the agent asks you to type "yes", takes a firm quote, stops if it is more than 3% worse than the preview, and refuses any recipient not in your contacts.


          

            Or ask someone else to pay: create a payment link
            

              

              
            

            

            
Prints a link to a payment page and nudges the amount by a few cents (or kobo) so the deposit can be matched exactly. With autopilot running (watch --execute), the agent pays the recipient once that exact amount arrives, within the same limits.
