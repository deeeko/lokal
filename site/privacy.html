This website

Nothing about you gets sent to us. There's no account database behind this site and no tracking code. Fonts are hosted right here, so no font company sees you either.

To show live numbers, your browser reads public data straight from Celo's network (Forno and the Blockscout explorer). GitHub and Vercel host the pages and, like any host, may log basic visit data under their own policies (GitHub, Vercel). On Vercel, a few small server functions pass along public explorer data and the exchange rate, and check sign-ins (below). They keep nothing about you.

Signing in with your wallet

Signing in means connecting your wallet and signing a short message that says which site you're signing in to, when, and when it ends (7 days later). Signing is free and sends nothing. There's no password, email or phone number.

  • What's saved: your wallet address, that message and its signature, in this browser's local storage under np-session. It's needed for the sign-in to work, so it's saved whatever you chose for layout. It ends after 7 days or when you sign out. While your wallet is switched to another account, the sign-in is paused, not ended: it picks up again when you switch back.
  • The check: on lokalpay.vercel.app (and the older naira-payout.vercel.app address), the message and signature are sent once to our /api/session function, which checks the signature matches the address and replies yes or no. It doesn't store or log them, and sets no cookie. On the GitHub Pages copy and the local preview there's no server, so the page accepts the signature your wallet returns without that check.
  • Signing out deletes the saved sign-in and disconnects your wallet from the site. To remove the site's access completely, also disconnect it in your wallet's "Connected sites" settings.

A sign-in only tells this site which wallet you are. It can't move your money: this site never holds money or keys, and every payment is sent from your own wallet, only after you approve it there.

On your device

  • Your privacy choice is saved so we don't keep asking.
  • Your sign-in, if you sign in (see above).
  • Your Lokal tag, if you're signed in: the tag your wallet holds is kept for 5 minutes in this tab's session storage, so each page doesn't look it up again. It's public anyway and is deleted when you close the tab or sign out.
  • Your layout (sidebar open or collapsed) and the last currency you picked (naira, rupiah or pesos) are saved only if you say "Sure".

The blockchain is public

Payments on Celo, including wallet addresses and amounts, are public and permanent for everyone. This site just displays them; nobody can delete them, us included.

If you leave a review, it's posted from your own wallet and stays public forever. Payment links include the recipient's name, the amount and any note, so anyone with the link can read them. Keep notes non-personal.

If you create a link on Get paid, your own wallet writes your name, the amount, any note and your wallet address to the Celo blockchain. Anyone can read them, with or without the link, and nobody can delete them, us included.

If you claim a Lokal tag (like @ada), your own wallet writes the tag, any display name and preferred currency you add, and your wallet address to the Celo blockchain. Anyone can look up which wallet holds a tag. Payments to your tag are public too. Releasing a tag stops it pointing to you, but the history stays onchain forever. Use a name you're happy to be public.

What the agent keeps

To actually send money, the agent keeps a few records on its owner's computer (never on this site or in the code): saved recipients (name and wallet), payment requests (including who paid), and a log of payouts for the daily safety limit. They're used only to make the payments people asked for, never sold or shared, and deleted within two years after the project ends.

So the site can show exact payment status, the agent also sends a short status note for each payment it handles to this site's /api/status: the transaction ids, wallet addresses, amounts and tag (all already public on Celo), whether it was paid, refused (with a reason code) or is being paid, and the receipt number. Notes are kept for 30 days. When you look up a payment, the page asks /api/status by transaction, wallet or tag; nothing about you as a visitor is stored.
